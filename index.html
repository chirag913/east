        Booking & the visit

        Do you actually come to my house?
        Yes — that is the entire service. We work at your home, office or job site anywhere in the East Valley. You never drop anything off and you never sit in a waiting room.
        How far in advance can I book?
        Up to 30 days out, seven days a week. Every booking gets a confirmed appointment time before we come, so you are not handed a four-hour window.
        How long does it take?
        A maintenance detail is about an hour. Interior or exterior alone runs 90 minutes to two hours. A complete detail is three to four hours depending on the vehicle. Ceramic coating is a four to eight hour job. The builder above shows the estimate for exactly what you picked.
        My vehicle is genuinely rough. Will the price hold?
        Usually, but not always. Heavily soiled vehicles, or ones needing the seats pulled to be cleaned properly, take longer than the standard time and are subject to an additional charge. We look at it and tell you the number before any work starts. There is never a surprise on the invoice.

        Services

        What’s the difference between maintenance and complete?
        A maintenance detail is for a vehicle already in good shape: premium foam hand wash, hand wax with a high-grade ceramic wax, vacuum, and a light clean of the plastics, vinyl, console and control panels. A complete detail adds clay bar as needed, wheels and tires, all glass, shampooed carpets and mats, spot-cleaned seats, leather cleaned and conditioned, steam-cleaned vents and console, and UV protection on every surface. The table above lays it out line by line.
        Do you do RVs, trucks, motorcycles and side-by-sides?
        All of them. RVs are priced at $25 per foot for maintenance, exterior and complete work, with interiors at a flat $399. Motorcycles, ATVs and quads start at $99, and four-seat off-road vehicles at $149. Pick them in the builder above.
        Can you get pet hair and smells out?
        Yes. Pet hair removal is a dedicated two-hour add-on at $99, and odor removal with an ozone treatment starts at $59. Both can be added to any detail or booked on their own.

        Protection & paint

        What does ceramic coating actually protect against?
        It bonds to the clear coat and shields it from UV, oxidation, dirt, bugs and tree sap, and makes the surface hydrophobic so water beads and rolls instead of drying in place. That last part matters a lot in Arizona, where hard water is what leaves the spots. It does not stop flying road debris, deep scratches or neglect.
        What is paint correction, and do I need it?
        Paint correction machine-polishes out swirl marks and light scratches — the haze you see in direct sun that automatic car washes cause. It runs $79 per hour. If you are having a ceramic coating applied, correcting first is worth it, because the coating locks in whatever is underneath.
        Will a coating fix scratches I already have?
        No. A coating goes over the paint as it is. If the paint is swirled or scratched, correction comes first, then the coating. We will tell you honestly which one your vehicle actually needs.
